One session a week, built to raise your jump and train your whole body. Basketball on Tuesday and Thursday covers the rest.

Heavy deadlift week

The session

Do it on Saturday after Friday’s rest. Tap “How to” on any exercise. The full session runs about 32 minutes. If you are short on time, cut the bench press first.

How it progresses over a year

Months 1–3: build the base

Learn the lifts. Squat 3×5 at 85–90 kg. Drop landings only, no drop jumps yet.

Months 4–6: add reactive work

Switch to drop jumps from 30 cm. Squat 3×3–4 at 100–110 kg, still paused. Add 2.5 kg whenever every rep is clean.

Months 7–12: go heavier and faster

Drop jumps from 30–40 cm only if your rebound stays close to your normal jump. Squat toward 115–125 kg for sets of 3.

Every 4th week: deload

Same weights, half the sets. You adapt while you recover.

Free technique reps

Before basketball, do 5 slow walk-throughs of your last three approach steps: long second-to-last step, short final step, then up.

Rules

Every jump is a max-effort jump. Stop a set when the bar or your jump gets slower. Rest fully between sets. Sleep 7–8 hours. Sharp knee or Achilles pain means no jumping for 3–4 days, then see a physio if it stays.
